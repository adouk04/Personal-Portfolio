Working method

Build with the model.
Verify like a human.

I use AI as a fast collaborator, then slow down around judgment: scope, claims, accessibility, edge cases, and whether the result actually helps.

  1. 01
    Frame it

    Define the user, the friction, and what “done” means.

  2. 02
    Make it

    Prompt, prototype, test, and keep a record of the turns.

  3. 03
    Interrogate it

    Check the output against the goal instead of trusting the demo.

  4. 04
    Reflect

    Capture what changed in both the artifact and my own approach.
